### Overtime Tracker v1.4.1 — Neo-Brutalist Home Screen Widgets Suite

This release expands the Neo-Brutalist design language to your Android home screen with two brand-new, tactile home screen widgets alongside the existing widget suite!

#### ⚡ What's New in v1.4.1:

- **🎟️ Concept 1: "Payroll Voucher" Widget (4×2)**:
  - Modeled after physical punch-card payroll receipts and vouchers.
  - Deep OLED dark background with a crisp 2dp white border and solid tactile Gold drop shadow.
  - Live cutoff status header (`[ 4 DAYS LEFT ]`) and active cycle subtitle.
  - Bold monospace Gold earnings readout (`₱0.00`) and rendered hours pill badge.
  - High-contrast Neo-Brutalist `+ LOG OT` quick-access button.

- **🏷️ Concept 4: "Rubber Stamp" Widget (2×2)**:
  - Ultra-compact, high-contrast lock screen and home screen companion.
  - Official bordered `[ APPROVED OT ]` stamp badge with days-remaining ticker.
  - Massive gold pay summary and total hours rendered.
  - Tactile circular `+` button for 1-tap overtime entry.

- **🔄 Complete 4-Widget Suite**:
  - The new Neo-Brutalist widgets are added alongside your existing widgets without replacing them:
    1. *Detailed Cutoff Summary (4×2)*
    2. *Compact Circular Progress Gauge (2×2)*
    3. *Neo-Brutalist Payroll Voucher (4×2)* — **NEW**
    4. *Neo-Brutalist Rubber Stamp (2×2)* — **NEW**
  - All widgets automatically sync in real-time whenever overtime shifts or cutoff preferences are updated.

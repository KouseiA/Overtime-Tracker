### Overtime Tracker v1.3.1 - Interactive Cutoff Calendar & 1-Tap Presets

This update redesigns the Payroll Cutoff Schedule settings with a visual, calendar-first experience and one-tap presets, completely eliminating manual number typing.

#### 🗓️ What's New in v1.3.1:
- **📅 Interactive Live Month Calendar Visualizer**:
  - Live monthly calendar map highlighting your active payroll cycles (Cycle 1 in Gold, Cycle 2 in Slate).
  - Clear boundary indicator badges on cutoff closing days.
  - Real-time "Today" tracker with remaining days countdown banner.
- **⚡ 1-Tap Quick Presets**:
  - **Standard (15th & End of Month)**: 1st–15th & 16th–End of Month (most common Philippine payroll cycle).
  - **BPO / Mid-Month (10th & 25th)**: 11th–25th & 26th–10th (standard corporate/tech schedule).
  - **Monthly Presets**: 1-tap options for End of Month (Last Day), 15th, 20th, and 25th.
- **👆 Visual Day-Grid Picker Dialog**:
  - Clean modal dialog to tap any calendar day (1–31) with dedicated `End of the Month (Last Day)` button.
  - Zero text fields or numeric inputs required.
- **🇵🇭 DOLE 3-Tier Overtime Rates (from v1.3.0)**:
  - Full support for Regular OT (×1.00), Special Non-Working Holiday OT (×1.30), and Regular Holiday OT (×2.00) with Night Differential (+20%) stacking.
